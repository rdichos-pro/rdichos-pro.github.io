Project Overview

This case study presents a complete finishes take-off and Bill of Quantities prepared from a set of 20 architectural construction drawings (Issue B — Construction) for a double-storey residential project in Victoria, Australia. All project-identifying details have been anonymised.

Quantities were measured directly from scaled drawings at 1:100 (floor plans, elevations) and 1:50 (internal room elevations), applying standard Australian estimating conventions. Openings ≥1.0m² were deducted from wall areas in accordance with AIQS guidelines; openings below this threshold were absorbed into the unit rate.

The deliverable demonstrates full competency across the estimating workflow — from reading and interpreting construction drawings, through measurement and assumption documentation, to producing a commercially usable, audit-ready BOQ with transparent methodology that can be reviewed, challenged, or repriced by any project stakeholder.

06

Assumptions Register

Every interpretive decision made during the take-off is formally documented below. This register forms part of the deliverable and provides full audit-trail transparency for the client, contractor, and design team.

A.01 · Floor FinishesAssumed

Porch excluded from hybrid floorboard take-off

No floor finish call-out on drawings. External transitional space. Bare slab assumed consistent with FFL differential (86mm stepdown). Dwg A03.

A.02 · Floor FinishesExcluded

Stair treads & risers excluded from floor area

Stairs priced separately per tread/riser rate. Including in m² would overstate area and risk double-counting. Dwg A03, A18.

A.03 · Floor FinishesConfirmed

Garage floor — concrete broom finish. No ceiling.

Drawings specify 'concrete' for garage. Open to floor structure — no ceiling lining called out. Dwg A03, A08.

A.04 · External FinishesRFI Required

Parapet finish assumed as face brickwork

Parapet height aligns with GF brickwork zone. No explicit call-out on elevation. RFI 001 raised. Dwg A04, A07.

A.05 · External FinishesExcluded

Parapet capping excluded from finishes scope

Parapet capping is a roof/waterproofing item — outside finishes scope. To be measured under roofing trade. Dwg A05, A07.

A.06 · Internal FinishesAssumed

No wall finish behind fixed joinery

Fixed OHC, base cabinets, built-in shelving and island bench are permanently fixed. Painter works to cabinet line only. Dwg A11–A16.

A.07 · Internal FinishesAssumed

Wall finish included behind freestanding appliances

Freestanding items (fridge, WM, DW, dryer) can be relocated. Painter finishes full wall. Client may change appliance size. Dwg A11.

A.08 · Internal PaintConfirmed

Openings ≥1.0m² deducted per AIQS guidelines

Standard Australian estimating practice. Openings <1.0m² absorbed into rate (de minimis). Applied to all wall paint items. Dwg A08 Schedule.

A.09 · Internal PaintConfirmed

Kitchen classified as standard paint — not wet room

Kitchen not classified as wet area under AS3740. Standard low sheen system applies. Splashback above cooktop = tiles. Dwg A11.

A.10 · Internal PaintAssumed

Laundry ceiling — moisture resistant paint applied

Laundry classified as wet area under AS3740. Steam & moisture from WM and trough. Moisture-resistant ceiling paint recommended. Dwg A11.

A.11 · Internal PaintCalculated

Stair soffit slope factor 1.276 applied

Rise 190mm / Tread 240mm per Dwg A18. Slope Factor = √(1 + (190/240)²) = 1.276. Applied to raking stair soffit area only.

A.15 · GeneralExcluded

Waste factors not included in quantities

All quantities are net measured areas. Waste factors (10–15% tiles, 5–10% floorboard) to be applied by contractor. Refer RFI 003.

Requests for Information

4 RFIs were raised during the take-off process. All are currently open — pending architect response. Resolution of these items will affect final quantities and confirmed pricing.

RFI 001Impact: Brickwork & render quantities

Parapet Wall Finish Specification

South and West parapet finish is not explicitly called out on elevation drawings A06/A07. Finish assumed as face brickwork consistent with the GF zone. Please confirm finish specification for parapet walls to allow accurate pricing.

Open
RFI 002Impact: Roofing / waterproofing trade

Parapet Capping Specification

Top-of-parapet capping/flashing specification is not shown on drawings. Please confirm capping type, material, and colour to allow measurement under the roofing trade package.

Open
RFI 003Impact: All tile line items

Tile Sizes & Client Colour Selection

All wet area floor and wall tiles are noted as 'selected tile' on drawings. Please provide the client colour selection schedule including tile sizes, colours, and brands to finalise quantities and confirm pricing.

Open
RFI 004Impact: Minor — confirm or vary

Kitchen Floor Finish Confirmation

Kitchen floor finish is not explicitly called out on floor plan A03. Assumed hybrid floorboard consistent with the adjacent living and dining areas. Please confirm or advise if a different finish applies.

Open
